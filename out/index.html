About Me

Hi! Welcome to my personal website.

I am now pursuing a PhD degree at HIT(sz) (VBASE Lab) and Great Bay University (EMAIL_Lab).

Before that, I obtained my Master degree from EEE at HUST, advised by Prof. Xiaojun Hei. I have got my B.E degree from CAU in 2022, advised by Prof. Hong Sun and Prof. Qiang Chen.

I am also member D789 of DianGroup, where i founded DianGroup Robotics Team.

I am eager to communicate and cooperate with people from all walks of life, and I hope you will contact me if you are interested! I want to make robots with real productivity.
